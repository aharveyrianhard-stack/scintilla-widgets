YouTube channels · connect to the sweep

A channel's videos come from what that channel subscribes to. To read a channel's subscription list the sweep needs that channel's own YouTube permission — one Google sign-in per channel, done once, stored by the collector and never shown here. A saved Chrome sign-in is not that permission. Press connect on a channel, enter the code Google gives you, and when Google asks which account, choose that channel, not the main account.

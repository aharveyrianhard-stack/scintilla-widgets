2 · The standing contract

Every extracted piece: own windowable URL · params on the card · data source named in-page · DB reads only, never FMP, geiger never recomputed · honest empty/stale states · ?bg=0 composed mode · receipt on the spine. No route may open unbounded nested pages — phones get a light path or an honest wall-only label. And every pane in a scene must be poppable — no second-class panes.

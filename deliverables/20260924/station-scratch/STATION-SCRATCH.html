A scratch screen, a to-do screen, and the timeframe always on show

Three things you asked for. An empty six-chart screen at the very end of the deck that you type tickers into. A to-do screen before it holding the four internals that are still somebody else's picture, each one saying why it is there. And the timeframe you are on, written where you can always see it.

Nothing is deployed. This is a branch. The branch, the commit and the command to push it are at the bottom. Every picture below was taken headless — no browser window opened on your screen at any point.

SCRATCH — the empty six at the end

"I need kind of like a one empty six chart screen layout kind of as the ending screen so that I can search tickers in and that it'll work and that they will change… think of it like if I was creating the layouts in new pages, and then in that new page we will rearrange, and I'll tell you, okay, this one we save."

SCRATCH is the last page in the deck. The right arrow from SCINTILLAS reaches TO-DO, then SCRATCH, then it wraps back to INDEX NOW. It opens with six empty slots and nothing in them — no SPY, no SNDK, nothing chosen for you.

How it works

  • Type and press Return. Tap an empty slot; its ticker box opens; type a symbol and press Return and the chart loads from the chart API, exactly like every other Station chart. To change a slot that already has a chart, tap the ticker name on the chart itself — the same control the rest of the Station uses.
  • Clear the box and the slot empties. On SCRATCH only. The chart comes down, the frame is taken off the page, and the slot goes back to "empty slot · type a symbol". Everywhere else an emptied box still puts the old symbol back, so a stray backspace on a saved layout cannot blank it.
  • One range control. The Station's own timeframe bar is it — there is no second one. SCRATCH is the one page that remembers where you left it, so tomorrow it opens on the window you were working in.
  • Remembered on this device. The six symbols and the range are kept in this browser (localStorage, wrapped so a browser with storage switched off still paints). They are not sent anywhere and they do not follow you to another machine.
  • It costs nothing when you are not on it. The Station only ever builds the page you are looking at, and an empty slot mounts no chart at all. The numbers are below.
  • A symbol the Hub does not carry is refused in words — "not charted on Hub" — never a black box.

"This one we save"

A copy layout button sits next to the timeframe bar and only appears on SCRATCH. It puts the wall on your clipboard as one line:

{"scene":"scratch","tickers":["SPY","QQQ","","MU","AAPL","TSLA"],"range":"3h"}

That is the line taken from the run above, with the cleared third slot kept as an empty string — the shape of the wall is part of what you are saving. Paste it back and it becomes a named page in the deck. The button says "copied", or "copy failed" if the browser refuses the clipboard, and the line is printed on screen either way so it can always be read off and typed back. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The grey lines are gone — measured, not assumed

Those four panes were grey because we were painting them grey: the Station handed the TradingView widget our own line colour. I opened the real widget headless in four configurations and read the pixels it painted:

ConfigurationWhat TradingView actually drew
area + our line colour (what shipped)grey — zero coloured pixels
area, colour keys removedUSI:TICK drew red #F23645; NASDAQ:AAPL drew green
chartType "line" + our line colourred anyway — that type ignores the colour we send
chartType "line", colour keys removedred

So the fix is to stop sending the three colour keys and let TradingView colour the line by the day, which is your rule: up green, down red. Everything else stays ours — the dark panel, the grid, the axis greys. We cannot colour these four ourselves: ADD, TICK, CUMTICK and TRIN are exactly the symbols the chart API does not serve, so we have no close of our own to judge the day by. That is why they are TradingView pictures in the first place.

The timeframe, where you can always see it

"the iOS style dock… I'm on the three hour. I kind of do need to know while I'm on screen what time frame I'm viewing."

The active range is now written in two places, in the dock's own grey and its own type:

  • in the strip's right-hand readout, under "screen 22 / 22", while the strip is down;
  • on a small chip outside the strip, which is the answer once the strip tucks itself away. It lives outside the dock on purpose: a tucked dock is a moved box and would have carried the chip off the top of the screen with it.

On a phone the chip sits at the bottom right instead, where it is not on top of a chart.

One honest note on the measuring: a page with six TradingView widgets crashed the headless browser mid-window twice — once on TO-DO, once on the old INTERNALS FAST — and both numbers above come from re-runs that completed. Six of those widgets at once are heavy. That is an argument for them living on a page you visit, not on the page you sit on.

Off screen, SCRATCH and TO-DO cost nothing at all: the Station builds only the page you are on, and neither page exists until you walk to it. On screen, an empty SCRATCH is the cheapest page in the deck — six slots and not one chart mounted.

Where each number comes from

  • Every price line and every chart on SCRATCH and INTERNALS — the chart API, scintilla-massive-chart-api.fly.dev, the same source the rest of the Station uses. Nothing here writes to it.
  • The four TO-DO panes — TradingView's own symbol-overview widget (USI:ADD, USI:TICK, USI:TRIN). They are TradingView's numbers, drawn by TradingView, and each pane says so.
  • The six SCRATCH symbols and its range — your browser's own storage on this device.
  • The screenshots — a headless browser against this branch, with the chart API proxied so it accepts requests from a local address (it only accepts scintillahub.ai as an origin). The Hub's symbol list was stubbed with the handful of symbols typed in, TradingView was allowed through because whether those panes are grey or coloured is the point, and every other off-machine request was blocked: 42 of them, per run.

What could be wrong

  • The captions are cut off in a narrow pane. CUMTICK's reason ends in "…" at both widths. The full sentence is in the pane's tooltip, but it is not readable at a glance.
  • The clipboard can refuse. Some browsers only allow a copy from a real click; the button says "copy failed" and prints the line on screen when that happens, but it is one more step.
  • SCRATCH's memory is per device and per browser. A layout built on the iMac is not on the MacBook, and clearing site data forgets it. Copy the layout out if it matters.
  • The range is remembered for SCRATCH, but changing it there changes the wall — it is the Station's one timeframe bar, so leaving SCRATCH on 1D leaves you on 1D. Only the return to SCRATCH restores SCRATCH's own window.
  • TradingView's green and red are TradingView's, not Scintilla's exact two colours, and we cannot correct them without a close of our own for those four symbols.
  • On a phone the Station pauses panes to stay light, so a TO-DO pane can read "paused to keep the page light — tap to bring it back". That is the existing live budget, not something new.
  • None of this has run on your machine yet — only headless here.

What I did not do

  • Nothing was deployed, pushed or merged. No Vercel, Fly or Supabase command was run.
  • The X panes and the X bridge were not touched. The self-update and idle rules were not touched.
  • No page was removed except INTERNALS SLOW, whose two panes moved to TO-DO.
  • The chart's own drawing, the cloud ribbon, the weights and every other page's rows are unchanged.
  • SCRATCH slots cannot be dragged into a new order yet — rearranging is retyping, for now.
  • I did not add a second timeframe control; the deck's one bar still drives the whole wall.

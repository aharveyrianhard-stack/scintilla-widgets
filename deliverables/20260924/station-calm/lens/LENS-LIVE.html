CONTEXT LENS — a live second zoom on the same chart

Not a trip through history. The lens takes the opposite zoom of whatever the main chart is showing, on the same live bars, and both panes always end on the same bar.

Bars are the saved daily closes recovered with the M47/M50 cases (real prices, dates shown on the lens heading). The green/red of each pane follows that pane's own last bar against the one before it, which is why the two panes can disagree: they are looking at different windows of the same line.

1 · The oscillator: what changed

You asked "what is MRG?" and said it didn't look like what Indicator Lab last did. MRG was a 60/40 blend of RSI and Williams, pushed through the Geiger's stretch-and-cap maths. That's why it flattened against 0 or 100. The Lab's script says plainly that it does not do that. So the blend is gone. Every chart now shows one pane, drawn the way the Lab's TradingView script "SCINTILLA RSI + Williams MTF REVIEW V2 · Clear Value" draws it:

  • RSI 14, as is (0–100): solid lines for 3H, 4H, 6H, 8H and 12H, faintest to strongest. The Daily is a thick solid line, drawn last so it sits on top.
  • Williams %R 14, same timeframes, same pink, dotted. Its Daily is a medium dotted line. Williams normally runs from −100 to 0, so it's drawn 100 higher to sit on the same 0–100 scale. That's the only change to it.
  • The cloud behind them is RSI only: the range between the 2-day, 3-day, weekly and 2-week RSI. If any of the four is missing, no cloud is drawn.
  • Guide lines: 30 and 70 solid, 20 and 80 dashed, 50 dotted. No lines at 0 or 100, and nothing is cut off at 20 or 80.
  • Title: RSI + WILLIAMS %R. When you hover, you get one row and it never wraps. On a small pane it shows the Daily only (D R49.2 W85.6). On an expanded pane, or a chart opened on its own, it shows every timeframe (3H R54.8 W79.5 · … · D R49.2 W85.6). The hover shows Williams on the 0–100 drawing scale. The right-hand tag is the only place its real negative value appears.

The same single pane appears everywhere: the Station's 8-up, a chart opened alone, the deck's ⤢, and the Hub company chart both collapsed and on EXPAND. The three stacked panes, the orchid line, the cyan Williams pane and the "MRG" tag are all gone.

Where the numbers come from

Both come from the same bars the RSI already used (the chart API, still-forming bars included, as since 1 Oct). The RSI calculation is unchanged: the S6 numbers match the old readout to the digit (SPY 3H RSI 69.7 before and after). Williams is TradingView's own formula, 100 × (close − highest high) ÷ (highest high − lowest low) over 14 bars, plus 100. Example: SPY 3H was "W−1.2" in the old readout and is drawn at 98.8 now.

2 · The Hub board opens on ★ FAVORITES

The board now opens on ★ FAVORITES instead of ♥ LIKED. If FAVORITES turns out to be empty once the lists load, it opens on LIKED, and on ALL if LIKED is empty too. You never see an empty FAVORITES board first. On a first visit it says "reading FAVORITES …" for a moment. After that, this browser remembers your lists, so FAVORITES appears immediately. Once you pick a tab yourself, it stays where you put it. The map, rotation and Geiger panels follow whichever tab is selected, as before ("47 tickers · FAVORITES" below).

3 · Blanks sort last, both ways

"Empty last" means this: when you sort a column, names with a dash in that column go to the bottom, whether you sort highest-first or lowest-first. Before, a dash counted as the lowest value, so sorting lowest-first opened on a block of dashes. This now applies to every sortable column (Last, Chg, F P/E, Mkt Cap, Revenue, RSI, Trend, Momentum, Geiger, RVol) on the normal board, full screen and the phone, which all share one order. The company list beside the expanded view already worked this way.

What could be wrong

  • On the deck's ⤢ view, the deck's "›" next-chart arrow sits over the right end of the Williams tag. The arrow belongs to the deck, so I didn't move it.
  • On a narrow pane there isn't room for the two tags to the right of the newest bar, so they sit over the newest stretch of lines, one above the other. The Williams tag always ends further right and never overlaps the RSI tag.
  • On a flat 14-bar stretch (high = low), Williams draws nothing, as TradingView does. S6 drew −50 there.
  • Station charts on a phone show no oscillator at all. That's unchanged and deliberate: the fan is hidden at phone width.
  • The remembered lists live in this browser only. If you add favorites on another device, the first server read (about a second) brings the board back to FAVORITES.

What I did not do

  • I didn't push, deploy or touch TradingView, and I wrote nothing under INDICATOR_LAB/ (the Lab's script and screenshot were only read).
  • I didn't add a way to see RSI and Williams in separate panes. You said TradingView is where you'd do that.
  • The screenshots ran headless on this Mac against the live chart API. The chart API only accepts scintillahub.ai, so node fetched it with that origin and handed it to the test browser. Every write request was blocked.

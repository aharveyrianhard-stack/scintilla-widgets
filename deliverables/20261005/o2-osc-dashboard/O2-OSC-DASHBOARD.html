2 · The rules, one line each

Adopted on this branch

  1. The Williams chip says what the Lab's says: W%R D −6.6, the Williams value alone. (Before: W D 93.4 · %R −6.6, the text the Lab had dropped on 2 Oct.)
  2. A dot is as long as its line is wide: the Daily Williams now reads as a row of dots, as on the Lab's pane. The thin Williams lines are unchanged.
  3. The pane carries its numbers: 70 and 30 are printed on their bands at the right edge, in the bands' own pink. A number is left off if a chip would sit on it.

Already the same as the Lab (live since 2 Oct, checked again line by line against the Lab's script)

  • One pink for everything. No grey line anywhere in the pane.
  • Line weight: 3H, 4H, 6H, 8H, 12H are thin; the Daily RSI is the thick solid line, drawn last, on top.
  • The ladder: slower lines are more solid (3H 54%, 4H 57%, 6H 60%, 8H 63%, 12H 66%, Daily 90%).
  • RSI solid, Williams dotted, the same timeframes for both.
  • The bands: 30 and 70 solid, 20 and 80 dashed, 50 dotted.
  • The cloud is RSI only (the range of the 2-day, 3-day, weekly and 2-week RSI). It is not a fill between RSI and Williams, on the Lab or here. If one of the four is missing there is no cloud (CBRS today: weekly and 2-week are missing, and the pane says so).
  • No dots at the newest value on either; the two chips mark it, each at the exact height of its line.

Not adopted, and why

  • The Lab's 20 to 80 focus. Since 3 Oct the Lab keeps its pane cropped to 20–80, so readings above 80 or below 20 run off the pane. Today's room rule (CH1, live since 15:48) does the opposite: the pane always holds the 30 and 70 bands and stretches to whatever the lines reach, so nothing is cut. The brief says keep CH1. This is your call (below).
  • The pane's height. The Lab's pane is about 16% of its screen; the Station's is 26% of the chart. Left alone: the height is a Station layout rule shared by every chart.
  • The Lab's bigger chips. Chip size here follows the chart's own type sizes; not changed by eye.
  • "Locked sources · sampled to view". The Lab prints this sentence inside its pane. Your 3 Oct rule is that explanatory sentences never sit inside a panel.
  • Chips switched off. The Lab's clean rotation view hides the two chips. Kept on here: they are the only place the numbers show without hovering.

5 · What could be wrong

  • The before and after pictures were taken about fifteen minutes apart after the close, so prices differ by cents (SPY 774.84 then 774.97). The oscillator readings are the same in both sets.
  • The Lab's pictures are weekly and 2-week charts; the Station's are daily and hourly. The drawing rules are compared, not the same bars.
  • On a very narrow pane the 70 or 30 can be left off when a chip sits on that spot. That is by design.
  • No phone pictures of the chart on this branch; the change is three drawing rules inside the pane and the pane's size and place did not move.

6 · Decision for you

Should the pane focus on 20 to 80 like the Lab's, or keep showing everything the lines reach? Recommendation: keep today's rule. On 5 Oct you asked for the pane to set its boundaries so it always looks clean; a fixed 20–80 crop cuts a reading of 93 off the top, which is the reading you most want to see.

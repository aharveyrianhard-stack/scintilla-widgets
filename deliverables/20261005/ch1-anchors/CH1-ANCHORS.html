The chart's context lens and oscillators anchor themselves

CH1, 5 Oct 2026. Station branch station/ch1-dynamic-anchors-20261005 from live b9feb36. Not deployed. Nothing here is on the Hub or the Station until you say so.

In one line: the little oval (the context lens) was always drawn 32 pixels tall whatever the move inside it, so CBRS's jump looked like a flat line. Now the lens, the oscillator pane and the price pane each size their own room to what they hold, every refresh, and nothing is ever cut off.

  • The lens was wrong the way you said. A +9% window and a flat one were the same sliver. That is a fixed boundary where you asked for a dynamic one.

2 · The four rules, in plain words

  1. The lens frames its own window. Its candles' own low and high set its plot (a small pad, re-fitted on every refresh, never the big chart's scale), and the plot is as tall as the move: six pixels for every 1% the window spans, never thinner than it was before (28–32 px), never taller than 30% of the oval's length or 28% of the chart's plot. A quiet day stays a slim sliver; a +38% day stands up. A taller oval that cannot find an empty dark patch steps down in height rather than disappear.
  2. The oscillator pane keeps its numbers and spends its room where the lines are. RSI and Williams %R stay on their natural 0–100; the pane always shows the 30 and 70 bands, and stretches from there only as far as the visible lines and cloud go (plus 4), never past 0 or 100. A window that lives in 40–60 fills the pane instead of a fifth of it; a reading of 97 is drawn at 97 with air above it, never pinned on the frame. A band that lies outside the room (20 or 80) is simply not drawn.
  3. The price pane's room is the visible window and its clouds. The y range fits the bars on screen and the cloud ribbon under them (a cloud above the price used to run off the top), then the usual margins (6% under, 8% over), and never goes below zero. The window is never widened to make a move look smaller: a +38% day on a hundred-day chart is exactly as big as it is.
  4. The Geiger readout and the labels never sit on the trace. The Geiger chip takes an empty spot above the plot, the cloud names live in their own band at the right edge, the lens takes the emptiest dark space and keeps out of the chip and the badge. This is measured, not assumed: every picture below was checked by the harness for anything outside its canvas, any oscillator value outside its room, and any chip, lens or label on the price line.

4 · What could be wrong, what I did not do

  • The lens's full height (up to 28% of the plot) is bigger than the 27 Sep "quite a bit smaller" oval on names whose window spans a lot (CBRS's sixty days, SNDK). That is the rule working; if it is too much, the one dial is PX_PER_PCT (6 px per 1%) or the caps (30% of the length, 28% of the plot).
  • The +38.35% is not explained. Both hosts and the board compute today's change off the chart API's settled 2 Oct close; nothing I can read gives +38%. If it shows again, a picture with the time will let the coordinator trace which read produced it.
  • The 3D and 1W series ending on 30 Sep / 27 Sep is the provider's series, not touched here.

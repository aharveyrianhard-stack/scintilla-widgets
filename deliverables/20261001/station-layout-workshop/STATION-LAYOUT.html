Station · the layout workshop: X as a column, the video trimmed, more charts, a messy chart

Blueprints only. Branch station/layout-workshop-20261001, built on today's live Station (75e653c). Nothing live changes, nothing is pushed, nothing is deployed. The three blueprints are real pages built from the Station's own chart, video and X panes, arranged in a different order. The deck itself was not edited. Every picture here was taken in a hidden browser at 1680×1050, 2560×1440, iPad (1024×768) and phone (390 wide) on 1 Oct between 10:10 and 10:40 New York.

In one minute
  • Why one post. Today the X pane is 839 px wide and 495 px tall at 1680×1050. The capture of X's 600 px column is blown up 1.37× to fill that width, so X's text lands at about 21 px and one post with a picture fills the whole pane. At 2560×1440 it is 2.1× and 32 px text: not even one post fits.
  • Blueprint A (recommended): X as a left column 454 px wide and the full 1050 tall. Text at 11 px (the floor), 4 posts fully visible, 6 partly. It advances one post at a time and pauses while your pointer is on it. The video keeps a true 16:9 picture with no bars, 14% narrower than today (715 vs 830 px). The charts area holds the same 6 charts plus the messy chart, which is yours: type a ticker, pick a timeframe, pin it, and it remembers.
  • Blueprint B is the mirror (X as a right rail). Same numbers; the difference is what sits next to what.
  • Blueprint C puts the video on top of X in one left column. Biggest charts (5 + messy at 391×525 instead of 6 at 559×277), but the video drops to 504 px wide and only 1 post is fully visible, because the column is wider and the text bigger.
  • The black bars on the video are small at 1680×1050 (5 px a side) and real at 2560×1440 (62 px a side), because the video pane is half the row by your 23 Sep call ("Let's just give the YouTube 50-50"). Every blueprint sizes the pane to its picture, so the bars go to 0 at any screen.
  • Speed. The X source scrolls at the slider's px/s (default 3, cap 30) in the X tab, and the pane magnifies that by its scale: 1.7× today at 1680, 2.6× at 2560. In the column it becomes 0.9× and 1.26×. The saved slider value lives in your Chrome's extension storage; I did not read it.

3 · The messy chart

  • What it is. One chart pane that is yours. A small bar on top: a ticker box (click it, type, Enter), the timeframes 15M 30M 1H 2H 3H 1D 1W, and PIN. The chart below is the Station's own chart shell, so it has the clouds, the Geiger chip, the lens and the crosshair work of lane S5 when that merges.
  • What it remembers. Ticker, timeframe and whether it is pinned, per browser, under the key station.messy.v1, the same way the Station remembers your scene and rotation. Tested: typed AVGO, chose 3H, pressed PIN, and the browser's memory read {"t":"AVGO","range":"3H","pinned":true} with the pane loading t=AVGO&range=3H. Moving this to the shared Station state (so the iPad and the display window see the same messy chart) is a later step, not in these blueprints.
  • Pinned means: rotation and page changes leave this pane alone. Unpinned, the slot could follow the page like the others. (The blueprint shows the control; the deck does not yet know the word.)
  • Where it sits. A: bottom of the right block, beside the video, 509 × 430 at 1680. B: the same strip, mirrored. C: the last slot of the chart block, the same size as every chart (391 × 525 at 1680). iPad: a chart slot in all three.

5 · Recommendation and the decisions

Build A. It is what you described, it gets the posts from 1 to 4, it removes the black bars at every screen, it adds the messy chart without dropping a chart, and the charts-only mode from lane S5 still works on top of it (it hides the column and the strip and gives the charts the wall). B is A with the column on the other side and costs nothing to offer as a toggle later. C is the charts-first option and would be the right one only if the video can be much smaller.

  1. Left or right? (A or B.) Recommendation: A, left, as you asked. Keep B in the back pocket as a per-browser toggle; it is the same code.
  2. How much video? The blueprint gives the bottom strip 41% of the right block's height, which makes the picture 715 px wide at 1680 (−14%). Giving the strip 45% makes it about 780 (−6%, "a little smaller") and takes 40 px of height from the charts. Recommendation: the 45% strip. It matches your words and the charts still gain the messy chart.
  3. How wide is the column? At 1680 the column is 454 px, the narrowest that keeps X's text at 11 px; at 2560 it is 620, which shows X at its own size. Wider means bigger text and fewer charts' pixels; narrower breaks the text floor. Recommendation: as built, and capture the X source tab at 100% zoom instead of 125% so the column is not a shrunk picture of a magnified page.

What could be wrong

  • Posts visible is a model, not a count of real posts. It uses X's geometry (a text post about 215 px tall, a post with a picture about 560 px, in the 600 px column at 100%) and the real column widths. Long threads, quote posts and videos are taller; a feed of short posts shows more. The "1 post today" matches what you see, which is the check I have.
  • The column is a downscaled video stream. The X capture is a picture of a 125% zoomed tab. Shrinking it to 0.73× for the column will make 11 px text soft. Decision 3 (capture at 100%) is the fix; it is a change to the X source tab's zoom, not to the Station.
  • Speed. I could not read your saved slider value, so I can not say how fast your tape actually runs today. The arithmetic above says what any slider value becomes on screen.
  • Video sizes assume 16:9. A channel that publishes another ratio gets YouTube's own bars inside the fitted picture, as today.
  • The deck's page arrows, the lens and the Geiger push are not in the blueprints: the panes read their own Geiger after 4 s (the shell's workshop-page behaviour), which is why the chips show. Building A for real means the deck's row model changes from two rows to a column plus a block, and the charts-only mode and the media expansion ladder need to be re-pointed at it.

What I did not do

  • No change to /deck, to any shell, or to anything live. No push, no deploy.
  • No real X capture in the hidden browser (it needs your Chrome and the extension), hence the frames.
  • No read of your Chrome's extension storage (the speed slider).
  • Tests: node --test tests/ on the live code gave 894 tests, 875 pass, 18 fail before I started (the route-inventory and youtube-live groups, failing on dates and surfaces from previous days). My 7 new tests pass; the baseline failures remain.

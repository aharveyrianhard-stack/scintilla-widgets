Station and Hub · the merged RSI + Williams oscillator

S6, 1 Oct 2026. Station branch station/s6-merged-osc-20261001 on live 18581fd; Hub branch hub/s6-merged-osc-20261001 on live 1aed0c1. Not pushed, not deployed.

In one line: wherever a chart shows oscillators, a compact pane now shows one merged oscillator — 60% RSI + 40% Williams %R, the Geiger's own momentum mix — and when the chart gets the room (the Station's ⤢ expand, a chart opened alone, the Hub's EXPAND) it shows three stacked: merged, RSI (the approved pink), Williams %R (the Hub's cyan).

Alan, 1 Oct: "the merged oscillators, RSI and Williams alone without stochastic, I think it would look pretty clean… adding the merge of both with a slightly different colour; wherever we have oscillators on Station and on Hub present the merged; as I expand into more real estate, present the individuals."

Before and after

What you will see

Compact charts — the merged one

The Station's multi-chart walls (8-up, 2-up, every RSI page) and the Hub's company chart (not expanded) show one pane titled MERGED RSI+%R 60/40. It is built just like the pink RSI pane you approved: five dotted lines (3H, 4H, 6H, 8H, 12H), one thick solid daily line, a slow cloud behind them (2D, 3D, W, 2W), the 30/70 guides and a tag on the daily value ("MRG D 40.7"). The colour is an orchid version of the same pink, so it reads as a cousin of the RSI rather than a new thing.

Expanded charts — the three

Given the room, the chart stacks three panes under the price, sharing its dates and its crosshair: merged on top, then RSI (exactly the approved pink pane), then Williams %R in cyan on its own −100…0 scale with −20 and −80 guides (Williams' usual "overbought / oversold" lines). Each pane has its title top-left and its daily tag at the right.

Hover

Hovering puts the date line through the price and every pane. Each pane's title row then reads every timeframe at that date. On the merged pane each timeframe shows its merged value and both ingredients, e.g. 3H 70.1 R54.8 W−20.5 = merged 70.1, from RSI 54.8 and Williams −20.5.

Where each number comes from

  • Bars: the chart API (Massive), the same reads the RSI pane already made, per source timeframe. Each bar now keeps its high and low as well as its close (Williams needs the range). Widths the API lacks are built from smaller bars as before (8H = two 4H, etc.); a built bar takes the highest high and lowest low of its pieces.
  • RSI(14): unchanged — the same Wilder RSI the pink pane draws.
  • Williams %R(14): where the close sits in the last 14 bars' high–low range: 0 at the top, −100 at the bottom; a flat range gives −50 (the Geiger publisher's rule).
  • Merged: the Geiger's momentum, copied from the publisher (geiger-publish-artifact.mjs, provider 2cce315; mix 0.6/0.4 from the Equalizer snapshot): RSI 23 → −1 … 77 → +1, Williams −90 → −1 … −10 → +1 (each capped at ±1), then 60% + 40%. Drawn on 0–100 as (value + 1) × 50, so 50 = neutral and it sits with the RSI's 30/70 guides.

On a short history they could differ by a few tenths (Alan's "apples to apples" point).

What could be wrong

  • The merged line is busier than "pretty clean". Williams swings much harder than RSI, and the Geiger's maps stretch both to the full range and cap them, so the merged line often presses flat on 0 or 100 and its cloud sits pinned at the top. That is the Geiger's math as it stands, drawn faithfully, not a drawing bug.
  • Developing values: the code passes the forming bar through, but the chart API is not serving a forming daily bar right now (asked with forming=1 at 13:00 ET it returned completed days only), so every daily tag says "confirmed", before and after alike.
  • The compact-pane hover readout is three rows deep and covers the lines while you hover.
  • A source bar with no high or low gets no Williams value (never filled in from closes), so that stretch of the merged line is blank.

What I did not do

  • Did not push or deploy anything. Did not change the Geiger, the RSI arithmetic, the clouds or the Indicator Lab.
  • Did not add Stochastic or any signal line. Did not add Williams anywhere else on the Hub.
  • The Station's detail screens (DETAIL · SPY etc., a different shell) are not changed. The wall on a phone still hides the oscillator as it did (only a chart opened alone shows it there).
  • The Hub's hover screenshot did not land the pointer on the chart, so hover is shown on the Station only (it is the same pane).

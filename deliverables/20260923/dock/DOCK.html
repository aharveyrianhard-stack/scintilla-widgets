3 · Mid-magnify: the arc

The one trade-off, said plainly. With no sliding and a 2× chip, the pointed chip covers the inner third of each neighbour (4H and 12H above). The Mac Dock avoids that by sliding icons apart, which is exactly what Alan rejected. If the overlap reads as too much, the two knobs are DOCK_MAX_SCALE (2) and DOCK_REACH (2.2 chips) at the top of the engine; nothing else changes.

8 · What changed in the code, and what did not

  • Markup: the same controls, ids and handlers, wrapped in sections; the visual order is fixed in CSS, so nothing had to move in the DOM except the five controls that went into the ⋯ panel and the two readouts that went to the right end. The symbol-guide list moved out of the dock so a tucked strip can never become its containing block.
  • Engine: dockArc() replaces the old Gaussian-plus-reflow layout. It is pure, counts the swell in chips (so a 108 px scene picker and a two-letter timeframe swell alike), stops at the section, and returns scale only. Ten offline tests pin it: 2× under the pointer, monotone falloff, section isolation, a wide chip fully magnified at its own edge, continuity for every one-pixel move, no horizontal output, no will-change, no white or near-white hex in the dock stylesheet, and the auto-hide contract.
  • Auto-hide: a chip keeps focus after a mouse click, so the tuck rule looks at keyboard focus (:focus-visible) and open selects only; a click anywhere else drops focus the strip still holds. Caught by the harness, which never saw the strip tuck until this was fixed.
  • Colour: the dock uses --ink3, --dim, --crk and one new hover grey #A9ABC2 (lightness 0.71). It never uses --ink or --ink2 (#C6C8DE). The orange "DATA · DELAYED" in the readout is the existing market-status colour, unchanged.
  • Untouched: chart/index.html and its twin station-shells/chart-v1/index.html (byte-identical, blob f57c7fd5); scene logic, rotation, screens, presets, favourites, the Equalizer and every calculation.
  • Phones: below 720 px the sections wrap, the labels come back, and nothing magnifies (a finger has no hover). Not screenshotted in this unit.
  • Tests: dock suite 10/10. Whole-repo suite: the base commit already fails 21 unrelated tests (route inventory, chart badge, iPad header, …); this branch fails 20 of those same tests and no new ones. The one dock-related failure that remains, "the dock loses no control", counts buttons against a July commit and already failed at de95670.

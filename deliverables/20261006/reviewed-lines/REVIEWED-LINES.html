What was read, and how many lines

Both V24 pack files match the hashes pinned in the Lab's manifest. 647 pack rows decode to 486 retained lines (186 horizontal pivots, 76 straight rails, 195 logarithmic rails and 29 fitted pattern rails such as SPY's weekly T29), 89 bracket pairs and 72 slope-strip rows. The 6 Oct Daily selections are listed separately: 41 items, geometry pending. Every line carries its record hash, the pack file and hash, the evidence file and hash, and the Lab's approval flags exactly as recorded; 51 lines carry an approved slope and 0 carry an attention-range approval (the SPY/QQQ attention ranges are plot formulas, recorded verbatim, not lines).

Does our extract match what the Lab's pane actually draws?

The Clean pane was on QQQ at 2W when read (over the debug port, read-only, nothing touched). The pane drew 121 primitives: 17 sloped strokes, 9 pivot lines, 46 level guides, 19 dotted continuations, 10 bracket verticals and 20 label ticks. All 24 drawn levels that can be compared match our extract exactly (to one part in a million). The two sloped strokes not compared are the T36 fitted rails, which the pane evaluates at its own live time. Three of our 26 QQQ levels are not drawn as separate lines because the pack draws exactly-equal geometry once (2W B2 = 1W B2 at 755.65; 3D P3 = 1W P1 at 733.81; 3D P4 = 1W P2 at 660.45). The other 18 names were not checked on the pane, because that would mean changing its symbol.

What could be wrong

  • Gold, silver and oil: the Lab reviewed spot feeds (OANDA XAUUSD, XAGUSD, TVC USOIL); our provider serves futures-continuous series (GCUSD, SIUSD, CLUSD). The lines are the Lab's; the price they are compared with is on a different basis. Flagged on every such row.
  • Equities: the Lab's charts are dividend-adjusted, the provider's bars split-adjusted. HEAT1 measured about 0.2% between them on SPY. Lines are drawn at the Lab's prices, not re-based.
  • Sloped lines on our chart are straight chords through the Lab's two anchors; the Lab advances them per source bar. They agree at the anchors and today; between them a logarithmic rail is sampled so it curves as the Lab's does.
  • "Today's level" for a 3D line counts three sessions per bar from provider daily bars; the Lab counts its own 3D bars. One bar of difference moves a line by one bar's slope.
  • States use completed daily closes over 10 sessions and a 1% "near" band. Those two numbers are choices, stated on the table.

What was not done

  • No table applied, nothing deployed, nothing merged. Migration 0026 and its rollback are written; the loader ran as a dry run only.
  • No message to the Lab. The review queue is a proposed format in our workspace.
  • Parity checked for QQQ only (the symbol on the pane). Pictures for four names at two timeframes, not all nineteen.
  • The 6 Oct Daily selections have no geometry yet; they are rows marked pending, not drawn.

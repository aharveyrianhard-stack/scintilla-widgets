Cohort Geiger · component
/cohort
one cohort's composite distribution as a horizontal gs meter bar — min→max band, every member a dot, mean = glowing thumb, median = notch, MAX/MEDIAN/MEAN/MIN printed thick · composite_staged tf=D × ticker_cohorts is_primary, never recomputed · params: ?c=CRYPTO (pin) · auto-rotates cohorts · ?dwell=10 · ?bg=0 · ?labels=0 · ?refresh=60 · click → /heat cohorts, title → /compare
Cohort Compare · component
/compare
every primary cohort as one vertical bar from the zero line (MACD-histogram idiom, leader wears the glow) — bar = median composite (?stat=mean flips), notch = the other stat, whisker = min→max, scale ±1 · same reads as /cohort · params: ?stat= · ?sort=value|name · ?bg=0 · ?labels=0 · ?refresh=60 · click a bar → its cohort geiger

Scintilla DCF V1.1 — DB-wired · Edge Score · Dispersion Score · 3-Stage Fade

Same ticker bar / slider structure as FMP_CAPABILITY_DEMO.html §3. Now leads with the actual projected financial statement (line by line, 5 years) before any valuation math — that projection is what everything downstream reads off. Then: (1) an Edge score (ours — not a licensed rating) that auto-sets a fade length, (2) a Dispersion score (ours) that auto-sets a margin-of-safety band, (3) the Stage II fade + Stage III perpetuity math — spot-checked against a published NVDA equity model's numbers to sanity-test the wiring, not copied from it. Comps, insider/congress trades, and the reverse-DCF solver stay in the fundamentals cockpit (scintillahub.ai/fundamentals) — not duplicated here. V1.1 (2026-08-05, C7): wired to the shared SCINTILLA DB — quote from live_quotes, revenue/mcap from fundamentals, beta/shares from company_profile, consensus revenue from analyst_estimates, risk-free from treasury_rates (10Y). Static Jul-24 numbers remain only as the flagged fallback baseline. Never FMP from this page.

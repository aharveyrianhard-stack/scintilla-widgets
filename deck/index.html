What is in each slot, and the rule that protects it

Chart 1 — SPY. Chart 2 — SNDK. Not a guess: those are the two charts on the Apple TV wall you arranged by hand. Swap with ?c2=TICKER, or ?c2=auto to follow the loudest favourite.

Bottom row — the Scintilla market feed, your personal feed, and X. The two feeds are the WHOLE /feed-a and /feed-b pages: the list, the picker, the player. Choosing a video works here exactly as it does on the standalone route — nothing was stripped to make it fit.

X is a dock, not an embed, and that is not a shortcut. x.com is login-walled and refuses to be framed by anyone. So the pane reserves its exact spot and pops your real logged-in window onto it — the same thing the X dock on the TV wall does. It pops like every other pane.

Every pane carries POP, FULL and OPEN. POP gives it its own window (on iOS a video hands off to the YouTube app, which has real system picture-in-picture). FULL makes it full-bleed inside the deck with a way straight back. OPEN leaves for that route full-page. Nothing here is ever more than one click from its standalone self.

Lose nothing. The PANES row above lists every pane. On a phone the deck stacks and the live budget PAUSES panes behind a tap — it never removes one from the layout or the chip row. Anything paused is one tap from being back.

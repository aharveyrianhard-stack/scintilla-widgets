What is in each slot, and why

Chart 1 — SPY. Not a guess: it is the left chart on the Apple TV wall you arranged by hand. Revealed preference beats my taste.

Chart 2 — SNDK. Same reason — the right chart on that same wall. Swap it with ?c2=TICKER, or use ?c2=auto and the deck picks the loudest name in your favourites right now (largest move in either direction).

Bottom left — the Scintilla market feed (/feed-a) and bottom right — your personal feed (/feed-b). You already made that call; I did not re-decide it.

Every pane pops. On desktop a pop opens that pane in its own small window — the crop you do by hand, as one click. On iPhone/iPad a video pane hands off to the YouTube app, which has real system picture-in-picture; a cross-origin embed cannot be forced into PiP by any script, so the app is the honest route. The player's own PiP control still works inside each feed pane.

On a phone the deck stacks, the second chart is dropped rather than crushed, and one video runs at a time — the other waits behind a tap. That is the fix that stopped Safari dying on the geiger wall.

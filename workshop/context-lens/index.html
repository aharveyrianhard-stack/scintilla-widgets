Every chart here is the Station's own chart pane, drawn from live bars off the chart API. The lens is the small second view. Use the switch to compare the styles on all eight at once. Nothing on this page is on the live Station, and nothing here changes it.

Where the bars come from. Each pane is /chart/ in pane mode, loading its own daily bars through the Station's provider — the same route the Station uses. No second data path was opened for this page.

Why these eight. They were picked from live daily bars this morning so the wall is not eight charts doing the same thing: two rising, two falling, one flat, one that gapped, the index, and a put/call ratio, which is not a price at all. The numbers behind the choice are in picks.json beside this page.

The lens itself. Where it sits is the reviewed corner rule from 23 September (lens-placement.mjs); what it shows is the reviewed opposite-zoom rule from M51 (lens-view.mjs). Both files are byte-identical copies of the reviewed ones.
